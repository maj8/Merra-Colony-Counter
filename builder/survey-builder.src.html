Set up a seabird count survey

Give every counter the same photos and species list, and get back one set of counts, with the time each person spent on each photo. Free to use. Everything happens in your browser: your photos are never uploaded to Merra.

  1. Set upTitle, species, instructions and photos, below.
  2. Make the survey fileOne zip with everything inside.
  3. Send it outCounters open it at colony.merra.org/counter.
  4. Combine resultsCounters send their Save file; merge them here.

3 · Make the survey file

One file with your photos and settings inside. It is made here on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

Send it to your counters:
  1. Send them the survey file. Survey files are often too big to email, so use a file-sharing service (for example WeTransfer, Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox), or a USB stick.
  2. They click the link colony.merra.org/counter on a phone, tablet or computer, press Open survey file and choose the file. The survey stays on their device, so they can count without a signal. They do not need to unzip it. On a phone or tablet they can add the counter to the home screen.
  3. When they finish they press Save and send you the save file. Combine them in step 4.

To change the survey, make a new file with the same Survey ID and send it again: counters open it and carry on where they left off. Use a new Survey ID for a new survey.

4 · Combine counters' results

Ask each counter to press Save in the app and send you the colony_backup_….json file. Select them all here.
