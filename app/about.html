Merra Colony Counter

Count seabirds in colony photos, species by species. Tap each bird, get the counts. Free to use, on a computer, tablet or phone, with or without a signal.

What it does

Tap to count

Pick a species and tap each bird. Every bird keeps its dot, so nothing is counted twice or missed. Zoom right in on dense ledges.

Counts by photo and species

One master CSV of photo, species and count, plus every dot's position so the counted photos can be redrawn or checked.

Time on each photo

Records how long each counter spends on each photo, adding up across sessions and pausing when they stop.

Teach a computer to count

The training kit learns from the photos you have counted, then counts new photos by itself for you to check and correct.

Private and offline

Photos and counts stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded to Merra. Once opened, it keeps working without a signal.

Running a survey with a team

  1. Build the survey. In the survey builder, add a title, the species list, instructions and your photos. It makes a complete survey app in one zip.
  2. Publish it. Drag the zip onto a web host such as Netlify Drop to get a web address for it.
  3. Send the address. Counters open it, enter their name and count the same photos. On phones and tablets they can add it to the home screen.
  4. Combine the results. Counters press Save and send you their file. The survey builder merges them into one counts CSV and one time log.
