Colony Counterby Merra

Been sent a survey file? Open it here. It stays on this device, so you can count offline.

Or count your own photos: press Open images at the top.

Pick a species, then tap / click each bird to add a dot.
Right-click (or tap a dot of the selected species) to remove a dot.
Tapping a dot of another species changes it to the selected species.
Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Press Done when an image is finished.
